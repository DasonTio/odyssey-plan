## Purpose
`odyssey.md` is the single source of truth for a mentee's odyssey-planning work — not the conversation. Sessions get summarized/compacted as they grow, and a new session has zero memory of a previous one; this file is what survives both. Keep it as distilled state, not a transcript.

## Update rules
- Update at every meaningful checkpoint, not just at phase transitions — a dashboard score should be logged right after it's given, not reconstructed from memory later.
- Never delete a discarded plan, an earlier value, or a superseded next action — strike through or mark it instead, and log the change in the Decision Log with a reason. That history is often the most useful record of what the mentee actually learned.
- If the chosen direction changes after Build (new information, a next action that revealed something), don't just overwrite it — add a Decision Log entry and update the affected sections, keeping the prior reasoning visible.
